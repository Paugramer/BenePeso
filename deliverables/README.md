# BenePeso deliverables

Defense practice: [Word reviewer](BenePeso_Defense_Practice_Reviewer.docx) and [text reviewer](BenePeso_Defense_Practice_Reviewer.md). The twenty questions are ordered for rehearsal, with Taglish answers, short English alternatives, and follow-up prompts.

System verification: [defense readiness](Defense_Readiness_Check.md), [requirements report notes](Requirements_Submitted_Report_Notes.md), and [ban/log coverage check](Account_Ban_and_Activity_Log_Check.md). These are scoped developer checks, not a replacement for respondent UAT or a guarantee of complete audit coverage.

Research paper and revision documents are kept locally and excluded from version control.
